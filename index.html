If I could be honest for a moment... Sometimes I wonder how two people can share something that feels so meaningful, and then have to learn how to carry it quietly.

There are days when distance feels like the right thing, even when it isn't the easiest thing. Days when choosing what is right means leaving certain words unsaid, even when they sit so close to the heart.

You were never just another person to me.

And I don't mean that because of one particular memory or one big thing you did. It's the whole of you, the little things, the conversations, and all the moments that became special without either of us having to make them so.

I hope this new year of your life is kind to you. I hope you find happiness in the things you love, feel proud of how far you've come, and get to experience so many things you've been looking forward to. And I hope you get to see just how much good there is waiting for you.
